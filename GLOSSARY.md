# Glossary: A&B Revival (Assembly & Battle Space)

Ubiquitous language for the decompiled codebase, game mechanics, and modding architecture.

## Core Entities
- **Ship / Craft (`SimpleFly`, `FlyBase`, `FlyData`)**: The player's or enemy's spacecraft instance, composed of attached modular components.
- **Player Craft (`PlayerCraft`, `ConfigAndStory.PLAYER`)**: The primary spacecraft directly piloted and controlled by the player during combat.
- **AI Wingman (`WingmanCraft`, `AI_P`, `ConfigAndStory.AI`)**: Generic allied craft deployed from available player hangar crafts into tactical wingman formation slots (`0..4`).
- **Allied Flagship / Captain's Ship (`CaptainShip`, `special == true`)**: A unique, non-duplicable allied capital craft piloted by Captain Tom during story missions. Customizable and armable by the player in the Factory (`UIMakePlayer`), but strictly barred from Player control and generic AI wingman slots in `UIChooseFlyAI`.
- **Part / Module (`FlyPart`, `F4PartMC`, `BaseMovePart`)**: A discrete physical component attached to a ship chassis, such as wings, weapons, thrusters, or armor plating.
- **Attachment Point / Slot (`F4DataHole`, `F4DataLinks`)**: Coordinate-anchored socket on a chassis where parts can be connected.
- **Fire Port / Hardpoint (`FirePort`, `FirePorts`, `FireMode`, `FireSingle`, `FireLoop`)**: Weapon emitter logic defining projectile spawn points, rate of fire, recoil, and cooldowns.
- **Bullet / Projectile (`F4DataBullet`, `CkBltObj`)**: Projectiles emitted during combat with assigned damage, velocity, and collision boxes.
- **Shop / Hangar (`UIShop`, `UIRepair`, `UIMakePlayer`)**: UI and state management handling player assembly, repairs, part purchases, and hangar fleet customization.
- **Infinite Stock (`shopPart`)**: Inventory model for repeatable components (weapons, wings, engines) that never deplete upon purchase, cloning bought items directly into `plPart`.
- **Finite Body Stock (`type == 0`, `Body`)**: Finite inventory rule for ship hulls/chassis, requiring discrete unit counts in `shopPart` that decrement upon purchase and restore upon sale.
- **Purchase Stack (`Sel.`, `xN`)**: Staged quantity multiplier for a single shop item in `FormShop`, represented as `x1`, `x2`, etc., scaling total transaction cost and resulting in N cloned parts added on checkout (`ACCEPT`).
- **Hold-to-Stack Auto-Repeat (`FormShop`, `UIShop`)**: Rapid acceleration mechanism that continuously increments the purchase stack while the player holds the mouse button down on an item row, eliminating repetitive clicking.
- **Shop Reconciliation (`reconcileShopStock`)**: Routine synchronizing `shopPart` against `GIFT[0..STORY]`, deduplicating redundant infinite stock entries and backfilling missing historical unlocks in existing save files.
- **Battle Damage Telemetry (`UIGame`, `AIUnity`)**: Combat HUD subsystem displaying cumulative lost craft components (Guns, Wings, Engines, Modules) anchored above the player lifebar.
- **Ally Health Threshold Alert (`UIGame.checkCaptainHealthAlerts`)**: Tactical HUD notification broadcasting warnings when Captain Tom's total health bar drops past 70%, 50%, 30%, and 10%.
- **Critical Portrait State (`UIGame.updatePortraitCriticalEffects`)**: Visual distress feedback (pulsing red tint and impact jitter) applied to Michael and Captain Tom portrait frames when total health is at or below 30%.
- **Autofire Reticle Indicator (`OpOneCore.autoFire`, `UIGame`)**: Continuous weapon discharge mode toggled by key 'X', rendered as a mouse-anchored green circular indicator when active.
- **Combat Camera Distance (`RenderData.setCameraDistance`, `ConfigAndStory.CONFIG[6]`)**: Viewport scale state determining the battlefield field-of-view (FOV) rendered onto the 640x480 stage: Short (1.0x / 640x480), Medium (1.3x / 832x624), and Far (1.5x / 960x720).
- **Captain Tactical Mode (`AIUnity.tacticalMode`, `OpOneCore` 'E' hotkey)**: In-battle selectable behavior for the Allied Flagship (Captain Tom), cycled by pressing 'E'. Modes reset to Anchor each mission and are displayed via a center-bottom ephemeral HUD badge plus a two-letter code on the Captain's portrait.
- **Anchor / Hold Mode (`AN`)**: Captain holds his mission spawn point and fires at enemies in range (base-game behavior).
- **Escort / Follow Mode (`ES`)**: Captain trails ~80px behind the player, matching speed, defending nearby hostiles.
- **Evade / Flee Mode (`EV`)**: Captain flees from the nearest threat; once safe, holds position while remaining in Evade mode.
- **Seek & Destroy Mode (`SD`)**: Captain aggressively pursues and engages the closest hostile regardless of player position.
- **Fire Support Mode (`FS`)**: Captain maintains engagement range to its target and focuses fire on the player's target, falling back to the nearest hostile.
- **Shield / Intercept Mode (`SH`)**: Captain positions between the player and the nearest hostile (positioning only; no projectile blocking modeled).
- **Camera Viewport Scaling (`Cam.rect`, `RenderData.bmp.scaleX/Y`)**: Dynamic buffer resizing in `RenderData` that reallocates canvas bitmap buffers to match the active distance FOV while mapping mouse aim coordinates via inverse scale factors.
- **Camera Distance HUD Badge (`UIGame.showCameraDistanceAlert`)**: Ephemeral combat HUD text indicator displaying the newly selected camera distance upon pressing key 'C', auto-fading after 1.5 seconds.
- **Companion Heal Priority Navigation (`AIUnity`, `Targets.ia1`)**: Tactical AI behavior where friendly wingmen detect dropped repair items (`data[0] == "life"`) on the battlefield, making navigation to the pickup coordinates their primary steering goal while simultaneously engaging hostile craft in weapon range.
- **Hull Category Wing Capacity (`F4DataOrg.maxWingsForHull`, `F4DataOrg.wingCategoryForHull`)**: Assembly limitation scaling allowable wing attachments by ship chassis size determined by the hull name prefix: Small (`4`), Medium (`6`, including `B.Med++` variants), Large (`8`), Mega (`12`), with a fallback of `10` for unclassified hulls.
- **Assembly Wing Counter (`UIMakePlayer.initWingsInDtTemp`, `txtWings`, `updateWingsInDtTemp`)**: Telemetry element integrated directly into the assembly screen's bottom-right craft statistics table (`dtTemp`) as an additional row immediately above `Parts`, displaying live wing usage and hull capacity (`x/limit`) with vertical table compaction (`scaleY = 0.835`) to preserve the original visual footprint.
- **Wing Grandfathering (`F4DataHole.isMountedWing`)**: Backward-compatibility policy that permits existing saved crafts with wing counts exceeding the new category limit to load without part stripping, allowing repositioning of existing mounted wings while rejecting additional wing attachments.
- **60 FPS Modernization (`VeryOutShitABC`, `AbsMain.initSup`)**: Raising the target SWF execution frame rate from 36 to 60 FPS while rescaled frame-based intervals preserve original game pacing.
- **Dev-Mode Telemetry Overlay (`Fly4UI.text`, hotkey 'I' / '~')**: Runtime telemetry display reporting real-time FPS, frame delta time, logic/render execution durations, memory usage, display object counts, and live bullet/particle/array totals.
- **Unified Cleanup Seam (`Targets.reset`, `SimpleFly.startRemoveA1`, `RenderData.disBmpds`)**: Lifecycle management path that detaches parent references, unregisters active engine sounds, disposes large BitmapData surfaces on scene exit, and caps maximum particles.
- **Hardware Upscaling Layer (`StageScaleMode.SHOW_ALL`, `ConfigAndStory.applyResolution`)**: Window resolution architecture that keeps the internal rendering canvas fixed at 640×480 while scaling the display to 720p, 1080p, or Fullscreen without performance loss.
- **Domain-Hub Delegation (`F4DataLinks`, `F4DataHole`, `F4DataOrg`)**: Architectural refactoring pattern under FFDec CLI constraints where domain logic extracted from bloated UI monoliths is housed on existing domain entities, preserving the 156-script SWF ABC bytecode index.
- **Translation-Unit Helper Pattern**: AS3 modularization technique placing auxiliary, file-scoped classes outside the package block within the same `.as` source file, enabling clean separation of concerns without introducing unlinked SWF script symbols.
- **Mochi SDK Deprecation / Stubbing (`MochiAd`, `MochiServices`, `MochiEvents`)**: Complete neutralisation of obsolete Mochi Media third-party ad services (defunct since 2014) into zero-overhead no-op stubs, eliminating dead network startup latency and reclaiming binary space.
- **Fixed-Timestep Simulation Loop (`Fly4UI.SIM_STEP_MS`, `Fly4UI._simAccumulator`)**: Decoupled simulation architecture advancing game physics and logic in deterministic 36 Hz ticks (27.78 ms) while rendering frames at stage refresh rate (up to 60 FPS), guaranteeing authentic velocities, bullet travel, and timer pacing regardless of render frame rate.
- **Runtime Performance Quality Gate (`scripts/test_perf_runtime.py`, `flashlog.txt`)**: Headless/automated verification harness that runs the compiled SWF in standalone Flash Player Debug mode, captures per-frame telemetry, and asserts FPS and memory constraints.
- **Resolution Selector Component (`UIOptions.btnRes`, `UIOptions.txtRes`)**: Hallmark-crafted interactive options menu control enabling cyclic switching between 640x480, 1280x720 (HD), 1920x1080 (FHD), and Fullscreen display modes with native sound and visual feedback.
- **Adobe AIR Modernization Target (`HARMAN AIR SDK`, `application.xml`)**: Exclusive runtime target for desktop and mobile (Android/iOS), providing 64-bit architecture, native Direct3D/OpenGL/Metal acceleration via `renderMode=direct`, native gamepad/touch input, and modern platform compliance.
- **True 60 FPS Rescaling (`SIM_SCALE = 0.6`, `60 Hz Simulation`)**: 1:1 simulation-per-render architecture running at native 60 Hz, coupling $0.6\times$ velocity scaling with $1.667\times$ projectile lifetime extension and $0.6\times$ turn rate scaling to eliminate micro-stutter while preserving authentic combat radiuses and tactical dogfighting envelopes.
- **Stage3D Hardware Graphics Pipeline (`flash.display3D.Context3D`, `Stage3DQuadRenderer`)**: GPU-accelerated rendering layer executing underneath the Flash DisplayList, batching space combat sprites into GPU vertex buffers for 1–2 draw calls per frame (<0.4 ms) with hardware additive bloom.
- **Coarse-Grained Concurrency (`flash.system.Worker`, `shareable ByteArray`)**: Multithreading architecture restricting AVM2 Workers to decoupled asynchronous workloads (procedural map generation, asset decompression, 10 Hz strategic AI) while keeping per-frame physics on the main thread to avoid IPC marshalling stalls.
- **Surgical Blitter Optimization (`RenderData.render`)**: Immediate Phase 1 render pipeline overhaul eliminating redundant CPU blur filter passes, bounding trail-fading `colorTransform` to active entity rects, and blitting via direct `copyPixels`, reducing software render time from ~18 ms to <6 ms.
- **Mission / Director (`MissionData`, `Director`, `LevelData`, `LevelBase`)**: Campaign wave definitions, enemy spawn scripting, objective evaluation, and reward payouts.
- **Weapon Effective Firing Range (`F4DataOrg.getEffectiveRange`, `range`)**: The maximum theoretical travel distance (in stage pixels) of a projectile emitted by a weapon before its lifetime expires, calculated via the domain kinematic formula \(d = v_0 \cdot t + 0.5 \cdot a \cdot t^2\) using projectile velocity, burst acceleration, and bullet life from `F4DataFire` and `F4DataBullet`.
- **Weapon Range Metric Display (`Rng: <N>`, `FormShop`, `FormPlayerPart`, `FormRepair`, `ShowPart`)**: Visual telemetry displayed in weapon inventory lists and part inspection cards, allowing players to compare weapon ranges directly alongside firepower and power drain.
- **Unified 60 FPS Engine Baseline (`1:1 60 Hz Lockstep`)**: Single canonical 60 Hz engine loop where physics simulation and rendering execute in 1:1 lockstep (16.666 ms frame budget), permanently deprecating legacy 36 Hz simulation accumulators and dual-rate scaling adapters.
- **Native 1920×1080 Viewport (FHD Baseline)**: The game's internal rendering resolution and stage coordinate space operating at true 16:9 1080p ($1920 \times 1080$), providing an expanded tactical field-of-view without bitmap stretching.
- **Hybrid Stage3D Layering**: Rendering architecture where high-density space combat entities (starfields, craft, modular parts, projectiles, particles, additive bloom) are drawn via Stage3D GPU textured quads on layer 1, while vector UI menus and HUD overlays remain on the Flash DisplayList on layer 2.
- **Boot-Time Texture Atlas Generator**: Runtime asset ingestion pipeline that draws embedded sub-SWF vector definitions (`AllData_data.bin`) into high-DPI `BitmapData` and uploads them to a GPU `TextureAtlas` upon launch.
- **Normalized Socket Grid**: Grid alignment system in `UIMakePlayer` maintaining standard integer socket pitch and center-screen offsets in 1920×1080, guaranteeing 100% save-file compatibility for legacy and modern ship blueprints.
- **Edge-Anchored Tactical HUD**: Widescreen HUD layout system in `UIGame` that dynamically anchors gauges, radars, and telemetry to 16:9 screen boundaries (`stage.stageWidth` / `stage.stageHeight`).

## Unity 6000.6 Data-Oriented Architecture & Entity Mappings
- **Frozen Reference Baseline (`src/as3/`)**: The completed, read-only ActionScript 3 codebase and 60 FPS 1080p Stage3D build, retained exclusively for formula reference, state-machine verification, and vector asset extraction during the Unity migration.
- **Unity Production Target (`A&B-U/`)**: The primary and exclusive active development target running on Unity 6.6 (6000.6.8f1) URP, executing a multithreaded Burst-compiled zero-alloc simulation loop.
- **CraftAssembly**: Pure C# domain model encapsulating ship construction, socket coordinates, grandfathered wing bypasses, and O(1) incremental stat calculations (mass, health, thrust, energy draw) with strongly-typed `AssemblyError` validation.
- **CombatSimulation**: Pure C# Tier-1 reference runner implementing `IDisposable` with a zero-alloc 3-method interface (`Init`, `Tick`, `Dispose`). It delegates all formulas to the static `Integrate` functions and never schedules Unity jobs.
- **ScheduledCombatSimulation**: Unity-side adapter in `ABRevival.Presentation` owning persistent `NativeArray` pools and scheduling the Burst execution graph (`TacticalAIJob` → `ShipMovementJob` → `ProjectileMoveJob`) behind the same `Init`/`Tick`/`Dispose` lifecycle, completing each graph before returning and never leaking job handles.
- **PartCatalogImporter**: Unity Editor pipeline utility (`A&B-U/Assets/Scripts/Editor/`) decoding component definitions directly from legacy binary/XML data (`AllData_data.bin`), applying ADR 0012 weapon range formulas, and generating deterministic `PartDefinitionSO` assets.
- **Talk Script (`TalkScript`, `TalkScriptParser`)**: Pure C# import of the frozen `5_all.AllData_talk.bin` XML; dialogue lines keep their source index, and speaker ids are assigned in first-appearance order (0..12) so story cues and portrait lookup stay stable.
- **Talk Line / Portrait Speaker (`TalkLine`, `TalkPortraitCatalog`)**: One dialogue line with a speaker id, speaker name, and text; the portrait catalog maps that id to the matching `face` frame extracted from the frozen UI SWF (`9_all.AllData_ui.bin`).
- **ScriptableObject Part Catalog (`PartDefinitionSO`)**: Immutable Unity asset mapping legacy `F4DataOrg` static part definitions, hardpoints, firing parameters, and cost curves into serialized project assets.
- **Craft Blueprint Asset (`CraftBlueprintSO`)**: Serialized ship configuration storing chassis selection, attached module definitions, socket offsets, and grandfathered wing allocations (equivalent to `FlyData` / `plMc`).
- **Unmanaged Ship Entity (`ShipEntity` struct)**: Lightweight, blittable value type encapsulating ship position, rotation, velocity, hull integrity, and energy pools within contiguous `NativeArray<ShipEntity>` buffers.
- **Mounted Part Slice (`MountedPart` struct)**: Unmanaged struct representing a connected component instance (wing, engine, weapon) on an active ship, tracking local socket offsets and health without heap references.
- **High-Velocity Projectile Buffer (`ProjectileData` struct)**: Flat value-type record tracking projectile position, velocity, remaining lifetime, damage type, and collision masks, integrated by parallel Burst jobs (`NativeArray<ProjectileData>`).
- **PhysicsCore2D Multithreaded Collision (`Unity.U2D.Physics`)**: Low-overhead, multithreaded 2D collision detection pipeline processing projectile contact sweeps and hull hitboxes across worker threads.
- **Captain Tactical AI Job (`TacticalAIJob`, `IJobParallelFor`)**: Burst-compiled parallel job executing ADR 0006 tactical modes (Anchor, Escort, Evade, Seek & Destroy, Fire Support, Shield) over fleet entity arrays.
- **BatchRendererGroup (BRG) & Instanced Draw Calls**: High-performance URP rendering interface drawing hundreds of modular space combat parts in a single GPU instanced batch using unified Sprite Atlases (ASTC/BC7) and `unity_RendererUserValue` for per-part damage tints.
- **Zero-Alloc Gameplay Loop**: Architectural discipline prohibiting all dynamic heap allocations (`new`, closures, LINQ, boxing) inside `Update`, `FixedUpdate`, and `LateUpdate`.
- **Sound Catalog (`SoundCatalog`)**: Pure C# catalogue preserving the frozen `SoundData` effect and music ids, clip names, mixer bus assignments, semantic trigger names, and original playback volumes.
- **Runtime Audio Service (`AudioService`)**: Unity presentation service that preloads the extracted bank, owns the Music/SFX/UI voice pools, and reproduces the original music fade, duck and silence-floor behaviour.
- **Audio Mixer (`ABRevivalAudioMixer`)**: Minimal Unity mixer with Music, SFX, and UI groups, created by `AudioAssetFactory` and addressed by `AudioService`.

## Tooling & IP Vocabulary
- **Vargames**: Original independent game development studio and sole copyright holder of *Assembly and Battle: The Story in the Space* (2011).
- **JPEXS (FFDec)**: Open-source Flash decompiler and ActionScript bytecode editor used for unpacking, editing, and repacking `.swf` binaries.
- **REA (Reverse Engineer Anything)**: Agent-driven reverse engineering and static analysis toolkit connected via Model Context Protocol (MCP).
- **SWF (Shockwave Flash)**: Compiled binary container holding vector graphics, audio, symbols, and ActionScript 3 ABC (ActionScript Byte Code) tags.
